Billing agent service

The whole billing cycle, handled

Most clinics lose money in the gap between submitting a claim and noticing it was never paid. That gap is the job.

Claim submission

Your visits become claims and go to the Ministry on the regular EDT schedule — without anyone at the clinic touching a submission screen.

  • Health cards validated before submission
  • Code and premium rules checked in advance
  • Stale-dating watched so nothing ages out

Remittance reconciliation

Every remittance advice is matched back to the claims that produced it, so you know what was paid, what was short-paid, and what quietly wasn't paid at all.

  • Claim-level match, not just a deposit total
  • Discrepancies surfaced, not buried
  • Per-provider payout reporting for the clinic

Errors and resubmission

Rejections and error reports come back to us, not to a folder nobody opens. We work out why, fix it, and put the claim back in.

  • Rejection reasons explained in plain terms
  • Corrections and resubmissions handled for you
  • Repeat patterns fixed at the source

Revenue optimization

Each provider is benchmarked against statistically similar physicians in their own specialty, which shows up the codes and premiums the clinic is entitled to but isn't claiming.

  • Missed add-on codes quantified in dollars a year
  • Premium eligibility checked as claims are built
  • Rejection patterns tracked per provider

  • Exportable to Excel/PDF
  • Scheduled report delivery

Dedicated support

  • Dedicated account manager

  • Priority issue resolution

Referral analytics

See who actually refers to your clinic

The extension adds an IntelAGENT Analytics item to Arya's account menu, built from your own referral and intake data.

Referral volume per referring physician, with date filters
Onboarding follow-through — which patients never finished intake
Sortable tables with one-click CSV export
Runs live against your open Arya session — nothing to sync or import

Read-only against Arya

The extension never writes to your EHR — it reads the chart and writes only to IntelAGENT. It uses your existing Arya session, so no Arya credentials are stored, and opening the billing panel does not add a chart-access entry. Patient data stays between your browser and IntelAGENT's Canadian servers. Read the privacy policy.
