FAQ

Referral questions, answered

How exactly does a referral qualify?

A referral qualifies when the physician you refer signs up, selects you as their referring physician, and stays active past their 3-month free trial. At that point, six months of free billing is credited to your account.

What does my colleague have to do?

Just one thing: select you as their referring physician when they create their IntelAGENT account. There are no codes to enter and nothing to send back to us.

How many colleagues can I refer?

As many as you like. Each qualifying referral adds another six months of free billing, and the months stack.

Does my colleague lose anything by naming me?

No. They still receive the full standard 3-month free trial. Naming you as their referring physician costs them nothing — it only benefits you.

When do the free months appear on my account?

After the physician you referred finishes their 3-month free trial and continues as an active subscriber. Once that happens, we credit the six free months automatically.

Know a colleague who'd switch?

Start with IntelAGENT yourself, then send them our way. Every physician who stays is six months back in your pocket.
