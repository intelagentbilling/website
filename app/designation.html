Getting started

MC EDT designation

Before we can transmit your claims, the Ministry needs you to name IntelAGENT as your EDT designee. It takes a few minutes, and acceptance usually follows within 1–2 business days.

What you need

  • Your GO Secure account for the Ministry's EDT portal
  • About five minutes at a computer

Steps

  1. Sign in to the Ministry's EDT portal with your GO Secure account.
  2. Open Designee Maintenance.
  3. Click Add Designee.
  4. Enter the designee details and click Save:
    Full name: IntelAGENT
    Email: agent@intelagent.ca
  5. Click Confirm this Designation.
  6. Wait for the GO Secure email telling you IntelAGENT has accepted the invitation.
  7. Grant permissions: follow the link in that email back to GO Secure, open Designee Maintenance, click the agent@intelagent.ca hyperlink, select MC EDT, then click Select All and Save.

When it's done

IntelAGENT is designated as your agent and can submit claims to the Ministry on your behalf. Go back to Settings → Account Activation in the app and tap the button on the designation step to confirm it — we check with the Ministry directly.

If something goes wrong

If the designation step still doesn't confirm after you've granted permissions, tap Settings → Ask us a question in the app, or email agent@intelagent.ca and we'll check it from our side.
