In short

SnapBill and IntelAGENT are both independent, Ontario-only providers with capped self-service pricing a few dollars apart. SnapBill is cheaper on paper — 0.2% capped at $34.99 + HST against 0.25% capped at $39.99 — and is a web application. IntelAGENT publishes native iOS and Android apps with health card scanning and is built around hospital, group and departmental billing. Both offer three months free.

Everything about SnapBill here is taken from snapbill.ca on 11 October 2026. We have not rated them, counted reviews, or described capabilities we cannot source to their own material.

The price difference, honestly

SnapBill's published rate is lower. At a volume where both caps bind, the gap is $34.99 plus HST against $39.99 — call it a few dollars a month. Below the caps, 0.2% against 0.25% on $10,000 of paid claims is $20 against $25.

That is not a decision. It is noise next to what you spend on anything else in a practice, and both providers cap, which is the structurally important part. Choose on what the software does, and treat the price as equivalent.

The one place SnapBill's pricing genuinely differs structurally is the full-service tier: 1.5% plus HST against 2%. At $30,000 a month in paid claims that is a real gap, and if you want a billing agent it is worth weighing.

New graduates. SnapBill publishes a 50% discount in the first year for new graduates. IntelAGENT does not publish an equivalent. If you are in your first year of practice and the self-service rate matters at that scale, it is worth asking both.

Web app or native app

This is the substantive difference between the two products.

SnapBill runs at app.snapbill.ca and, as of October 2026, does not advertise a native mobile app. A web app on a phone works — it is a browser, and browsers work — but it is a different thing from a native app that opens instantly, holds a patient roster offline-capable in the UI, and uses the camera to read a health card.

Whether that matters depends entirely on where you bill from. For a clinic physician at a desk, a web app is fine and arguably better. For an emergency physician billing between patients, or a hospitalist on a ward round, the phone is the whole workflow, and that is what IntelAGENT's mobile app is built for.

Hospital groups and departments

SnapBill publishes a page for groups. IntelAGENT's group handling goes further into the hospital case: billing under a ministry group number, splitting the group remittance back to the physician who rendered each service including reversals, and departmental reporting across a physician roster. See departments and groups.

If your practice is clinic-based, this is not a differentiator. If you bill through an emergency group, a hospitalist service or an academic department, it is most of the decision.

EMR integration

SnapBill's own material describes OSCAR and WELL integrations as in active development. If live EMR integration with one of those is a requirement rather than a nice-to-have, ask them directly where it stands — "in development" is a moving target and may well have shipped by the time you read this.

IntelAGENT connects to Epic through a SMART on FHIR launch, and has a browser extension for Arya EHR. Different EMRs, so this is a question of which one your practice runs rather than which provider integrates more.

Which to choose

  • Clinic-based, desk-bound, OSCAR or WELL — SnapBill is a natural fit, and marginally cheaper.
  • New graduate watching every dollar — SnapBill's first-year discount is real money at that stage.
  • Hospital, emergency or group billing — IntelAGENT is built for it.
  • Billing from a phone between patients — a native app with card scanning is a different daily experience.
  • You want a billing agent — SnapBill's 1.5% is cheaper than 2%; compare what each includes.

Both offer three months free, and neither switch is hard to reverse. See switching OHIP billing software.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SnapBill cost?

SnapBill publishes an Essentials tier at 0.2% of paid claims plus HST, capped at $34.99 plus HST per month, and a Premium tier at 1.5% plus HST with no cap. It also states no setup fees, no minimums and no contracts, three months of Premium free, a 50% new-graduate discount in the first year, and a $150 referral credit. Checked October 2026.

Does SnapBill have a mobile app?

SnapBill is a web application, served at app.snapbill.ca. As of October 2026 it does not advertise a native iOS or Android app on its own website. IntelAGENT publishes native apps for both platforms with health card scanning.

Does SnapBill integrate with an EMR?

As of October 2026 SnapBill's own site describes integrations with OSCAR and WELL as in active development rather than live. Check directly with SnapBill for the current position before relying on it.

Is SnapBill cheaper than IntelAGENT?

On the published headline rate, slightly — 0.2% capped at $34.99 plus HST against 0.25% capped at $39.99. At a billing volume where both caps bind, the difference is a few dollars a month. It is not a large enough gap to decide on; the platform and feature differences matter more.
