The work

Two places money goes missing

Submitting a claim is the easy part, and it is not what you are hiring an agent for.

The claim that came back refused

A refused or reduced item sits on a remittance until somebody opens it. On full service that work list is your agent's, not yours — traced to the cause rather than flagged, corrected and resubmitted inside the deadline, and raised as a Remittance Advice Inquiry when the Ministry paid less than the Schedule allows.

The claim that was never entered

A premium you never claimed is never refused, so it never shows up as a problem — it just doesn't arrive. Questions

Full-service billing, answered

What is full-service medical billing?

A billing agent does the whole cycle for you: reviewing the codes before submission, sending the claim to OHIP, reconciling the Remittance Advice, investigating anything refused or reduced, and correcting and resubmitting it until it is paid. You record what you did; everything between that and the deposit is the agent's job.

How much does full-service OHIP billing cost?

2% of paid claims, billed monthly on revenue. There is no setup fee, no per-claim charge and no separate charge for working a rejection or filing an appeal — the software and the submission are included in the same rate. Because it is a percentage, the cost scales with the practice whatever its size.

What do you do with a rejected claim?

Investigate it, correct it, resubmit it and track it until it settles. You don't have to spot it first or ask us to chase it. If the claim was paid at less than the listed fee and we think that's wrong, we file a Remittance Advice Inquiry and follow it to the Ministry's answer.

Which claim types do you handle?

Fee-for-service OHIP, patient enrolment models such as FHO, FHG, FHN and CCM, alternate funding arrangements for hospital departments and academic groups, out-of-province patients billed through reciprocal medical billing, WSIB, private insurers, and private invoicing for uninsured services and patients with no Ontario coverage.

Do you bill for capitated and alternate funding practices?

Yes. Practices in a patient enrolment model such as FHO, FHG, FHN or CCM, and departments funded under an AFA or AFP, still submit claims — for the enrolled-patient record, for the work that falls outside the basket, and for the bonuses and premiums that depend on them. Your agent handles that the same way as fee-for-service.

Can I still bill from my phone?

Yes — full service adds the agent, it doesn't take away the software. You can capture encounters in the mobile app, the web portal, a spreadsheet or an EMR export, and see every claim and payment yourself at any time.

Can you take over from our current billing provider?

Yes. Changing the MC EDT designation moves future submissions to us; claims your previous provider already submitted keep settling to the same account, and we reconcile those remittances alongside the new ones. Tell us what's still outstanding and we'll track it with you.

How long does it take to start?

The gating step is the MC EDT designation at the Ministry, not our setup — allow a few business days for it to take effect. Everything on our side is built while you wait. For a group, no MC EDT designation is needed from the individual physicians at all: give us the roster.

Do I keep my own OHIP billing number?

Yes. The billing number and GO Secure account stay yours. You designate IntelAGENT to submit on your behalf through MC EDT, and you can revoke that designation yourself at any time.

Is full service cheaper than hiring a biller?

It depends on your volume, which is why the honest comparison is structural rather than a number. A biller on staff is a fixed cost that doesn't move when your billing does, plus coverage for vacation and turnover, plus software and an MC EDT account. Full service is 2% of paid claims and includes the software and the submission, so it costs less in a quiet month and never needs covering. Tell us your volume and we'll work out the actual figure with you.

Is there a contract, and what happens if I leave?

No contract and no long-term commitment — you are billed monthly and can cancel anytime. You revoke the MC EDT designation yourself, so submissions stop going through us the moment you decide. Your claim and payment data stays accessible for export after cancellation.
