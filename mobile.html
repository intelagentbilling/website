Features

Claims that go in right the first time

Four checks between you and a rejection, every one of them run on the phone before the claim leaves it.

01 / HEALTH CARD

The card is checked, not assumed

Every card is checked against the Ministry as you enter it — number, version code and eligibility. A card that would have been rejected fails here, while the patient is still in front of you.

02 / BILLING RULES

Every code checked against the rules that reject it

Nearly 200 billing rules run over the codes you have picked — limits, required diagnoses, codes that can't be billed together — and tell you before you submit, not six weeks later.

03 / FAVOURITES & SUGGESTIONS

The code you always forget

Your favourites and your last claim both sit one tap from a new one, and the app suggests the add-ons that belong with what you have billed — so the premium you keep meaning to add doesn't get left off again.

04 / REFERRING PHYSICIANS

The referring number is already right

Search a name and get the billing number from a checked list of Ontario physicians, rather than copying six digits off a consult letter and hoping.

Health Card Scanner

Scan health cards instantly

Point the camera at an Ontario health card and the number, version code and patient details fill themselves in. For a patient who isn't on a roster yet, this is the whole of data entry.

  • CaptureNumber, version code and date of birth in one tap
  • CheckValidated against the Ministry before you bill
  • A whole listScan a stack of cards in one go — each one validated as it lands
  • Then decideSave them as patients to bill later, or drop the batch straight into a roster
Revenue Tracking

Watch the month add up

Every claim you submit moves the number that day. Day, week, month and year, each against the same period before it, over a chart of what you billed and how many claims it took — so you know where you stand without waiting on a remittance advice.

  • ComparisonEvery figure against the same period last time
  • Two axesDollars and claim counts on one chart — a slow day and a cheap day look different
  • BasisPaid where OHIP has settled, billed where it hasn't
